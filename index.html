KettleCross U-Bell in the US

A kettlebell split into two masses, so your forearm sits in the gap and your wrist stays straight overhead. I protected the design (US design patent D1,001,923, plus UK and EU registrations) and licensed it to AbMat, who sell it in the US and Canada as the U-Bell in five weights from 8 to 24 kg.

Registered Fig. 2

Stirrup cable-machine handle

A cable-machine handle for people whose grip has gone, so they can keep training on the machines they already use. The hand passes through it. A padded saddle sits across the heel of the palm and the eye sits on the back of the hand, so under load it locks on and the fingers only close if they want to.

It's one aluminium casting with two soft pads and it clips to a standard carabiner. The design is registered in the UK (6553109 and 6553110, September 2026) and I'm looking for a licensee to make it.

Mobility Age

Four photos on your phone (squat, forward fold, overhead reach and lunge) give you a mobility age, measured against published clinical norms. Capture is hands-free with voice guidance and the pose model is accurate to about 4 to 6 degrees, against 10 to 12 on the version it replaced.

It's free to try and Premium is £3.99 a month. PTs and physios pay £47 a month to assess clients in the session, track them over time and print a report for each one.

mobilityage.co.uk
Live Fig. 4

Duty Diligence

A Shopify app for merchants importing into the US. It shows the duty on every product variant and what it does to landed cost and margin, checks the US tariff schedule twice a day and links the source for every rate. The coverage report is free and monitoring is $19 a month.

Forge

Printable desk accessories, starting with a MagSafe stand that took eleven versions to get right. 18 N52 magnets sit in a 57 mm ring to Apple's spec, the face tilts at 20° and it prints in just under three hours on a Bambu A1 Mini.

Background

At High Street TV I co-invented the FITT Cube, which raised £52,589 from 561 backers on Kickstarter. KettleCross and Stirrup came later and they're mine.

I use AI for the exploration and bring in professionals when decisions get expensive (patent attorneys, toolmakers, accountants).
